 Human continuity
 Human Continuity gives your application a stable way to recognize the same human across their accounts,
 devices, and agents while preserving privacy.
 Welcome people back, carry reputation forward, and create experiences that grow more valuable with every
 interaction.
 What you can build
 Build durable reputation
 Let credibility, contribution history, and community standing grow over time using a private
 identifier scoped to your sector.
 Protect scarce benefits
 Apply eligibility, claim limits, and participation rules consistently across every interaction with
 the same human.
 Power human-backed agents
 Give agent experiences a persistent connection to the human who authorized them, with fresh
 authentication for important actions.
 Recover established accounts
 Reconnect a returning human to their account in your application from a new device through the same
 continuity identifier.
 Enforce lasting consequences
 Carry suspensions, bans, and trust decisions across accounts, devices, and agents linked to the same
 continuity relationship.
 Private recognition
 Applications registered in the same sector receive the same stable identifier for a
 given World ID account. Applications in different sectors receive different identifiers.

 Fresh authentication
 Add fresh World ID authentication to your existing sign-in when an experience calls for stronger
 assurance. Ask the person to authenticate before a sensitive action, then check the result in your
 backend.
 Your application decides whether the authenticated person may perform the action.
 Use the stable identifier to recognize a returning person and authentication time to decide when to ask
 them to authenticate again.
 Powered by World ID
 People authenticate with an enrolled passkey or a World ID proof. World IdP makes the authentication
 result available through OpenID Connect (OIDC), keeping the underlying World identity and proof private
 from your application.
 OIDC delivers the private identifier as the pairwise sub
 (subject) claim. The application
 stores the issuer and subject together to recognize the same World ID account over time.
 For agent experiences, your application uses OIDC in the same way: it links the issuer and subject to
 its own account or grant, then issues credentials for its APIs or MCP server.
 Integration guides
 Keep your existing sign-in and add World ID for sensitive actions. For agent workflows, add a human
 checkpoint that requires fresh authentication before your application resumes the workflow.
